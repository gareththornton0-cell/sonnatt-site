Questions? Answers.

I don’t know anything about AI. Is that a problem?

Not at all. That’s why you get an account manager. We explain everything in plain English and do the setup for you.

What happens after I answer the questions?

Your account manager reads your answers and gets in touch to book a free 15-minute call. We tell you honestly where AI would help and which plan fits.

Can I start with just a website and chatbot?

Yes. Starter is £99 a month for a website and an AI chatbot. Move up whenever you want more, and everything carries across.

Is it run by real people or by AI?

Real people. Your account manager makes the decisions and does the work, using AI tools to move quickly and keep costs down.

Do you work with businesses across the UK?

We’re based in Leeds and work with businesses right across the UK: England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Everything can be done remotely, by phone and video call, so where you are makes no difference.

How much does an AI chatbot cost for a UK small business?

With Sonnatt.ai an AI chatbot comes with every plan, from £99 a month including VAT, together with a 5-page website. Agencies typically charge £1,500 to £8,000 to set one up, plus £75 to £500 a month.

What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist answers your business phone 24/7, takes the caller’s details, answers common questions and books jobs, so you never miss a call. Ours is a £149 a month add-on on any plan, and included in Scale.

What is the Sonnatt.ai AI advice line?

A phone number you can call to speak to one of our advisers about anything AI, for £39 a month. You don’t need to use any other Sonnatt.ai service.

Which AI tools do you use?

We work with all the leading AI tools, including Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Meta AI, Grok, Mistral, Perplexity, Midjourney and ElevenLabs, and choose the right ones for each business so you don’t have to.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

Starter is a 12-month plan, Growth is 6 months, and Pro can be cancelled any time. The AI advice line is a monthly service.
