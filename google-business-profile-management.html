Questions

Can you guarantee the top spot on Google Maps?

No one honestly can. Distance from the searcher matters a lot. What we can do is make your profile complete, active and well-reviewed, which are the parts you control.

Do I need a shop or office?

No. Businesses that travel to customers can set up as a service-area business, with the address hidden from the public.

Is this included in the monthly plan?

Yes. Google Business Profile management is part of the £995 monthly plan.

Will you reply to reviews for me?

Yes, in your tone, and we flag any negative review to you before replying.
