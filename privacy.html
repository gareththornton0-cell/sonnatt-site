Privacy Policy

Sonnatt.ai · Last updated 10 October 2026

Sonnatt.ai ("we", "us") is an AI consultancy based in West Yorkshire, UK. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights under UK GDPR.

What we collect

  • Details you give us when you contact us, use our website chat assistant or book a call: name, email, phone number, business name and your message.
  • Comments and messages you send to our Facebook Page or Instagram account.
  • Basic website usage data (for example pages visited, device type and approximate location), collected with Google Analytics, to keep the site working and improve it. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this.

How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and deliver our services.
  • To publish and manage posts on our own Facebook Page and Instagram account using our in-house posting tool. This tool only accesses Sonnatt.ai's own Page and account. It does not collect data about other Facebook or Instagram users.
  • To understand how people use our website so we can improve it.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

Lawful basis

We rely on legitimate interests (running and promoting our business), contract (providing services you ask for) and, where needed, your consent.

Sharing

We never sell your data. We only share it with trusted service providers who help us run the business (such as hosting, email, analytics and social media platforms), under appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Data deletion

To have any data we hold about you deleted, including anything connected to our Facebook or Instagram tools, email info@sonnattai.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will confirm deletion within 30 days.
