Frequently asked questions

Sonnatt.ai is a Leeds, West Yorkshire service that gives local businesses one dedicated account manager for their website, chatbot and marketing: £995 to set up, then £995 a month.

More detail in our plain-English guides: what a website costs, whether you need a chatbot, and setting up your Google Business Profile.

What does Sonnatt.ai do?

Sonnatt.ai builds and runs your website, a chatbot for it, any custom apps you need, and the marketing that brings people to it. One dedicated account manager looks after all of it, so you are not juggling separate freelancers.

How much does Sonnatt.ai cost?

There is a one-off setup fee of £995 and then £995 a month for single-site businesses, which you can cancel any time. Larger operations with multiple sites, locations or system integrations are scoped after a discovery session, from £3,000 a month.

What is included in the £995 monthly fee?

Hosting and running costs, marketing and social media management, a monthly review call and priority support.

What is included in the £995 setup?

A discovery and strategy session, your website and chatbot built and live, connection to your existing systems, and a team training session.

Which areas does Sonnatt.ai cover?

Sonnatt.ai is based in Leeds, West Yorkshire, and works with businesses in Leeds, Bradford and across Yorkshire. Most of the work can be done remotely, so businesses further away are welcome to get in touch.

Is Sonnatt.ai run by real people or by AI?

Real people. Your website, chatbot, apps and marketing are built and managed by a real team that uses AI to work quickly and keep costs down. The chat assistant on this site is automated, and the presenter in the introduction video is AI-generated.

Can the chatbot connect to the tools I already use?

Yes. The chatbot can be connected to the calendar, booking tool or CRM you already use.

What kinds of business is it for?

Letting and property management, accountants and advisers, clinics and practices, salons, gyms and venues, and trades and contractors, plus any other business that needs a proper online presence and some help running it.
