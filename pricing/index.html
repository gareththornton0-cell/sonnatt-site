How the prices compare

We set our prices against what UK small businesses pay elsewhere:

  • DIY chatbot tools cost about $24 to $40 a month (Tidio, Chatbase), but you set them up and keep them trained yourself. Managed chatbot services in the UK usually charge £150 to £400 a month.
  • Pay-monthly websites from UK specialists typically run £65 to £250 a month, often without a chatbot and sometimes with a 12-month minimum.
  • Social media management on its own is usually £800 to £2,500 a month from a UK agency, before your website or chatbot.

Prices checked October 2026.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Sonnatt.ai cost?

There are three ways to start. The chatbot on its own is £99 setup then £49 a month. Starter, a website with the chatbot built in, is £299 setup then £149 a month. Core, where we build and run your website, chatbot, marketing and social media, is £995 setup then £995 a month.

Can I sign up online?

Yes. The Chatbot and Starter plans can be started online with no call. We set everything up and email you when it's ready. Core and Scale start with a free 15-minute call.

What's the difference between Starter and Core?

Starter gives you a professional website and chatbot, hosted and kept up to date, with small changes each month. Core adds the marketing: social media management, review requests, your Google profile, a monthly review call and a dedicated account manager.

What is the Scale plan?

For businesses with several sites, branches or systems. It starts from £3,000 a month and includes a named account manager, same-day priority support and quarterly strategy reviews. The setup is quoted after a discovery call.
