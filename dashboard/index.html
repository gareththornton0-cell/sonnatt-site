Phone Script

Fill in the prospect once and every line below is personalised. Copy straight into a call.

Call flow at a glance

1
Open

Yes → Hook. "Not a good time" → book a callback or send Email 1 (log "Sent info"). "Not interested" → thank them, log it, move on.

2
Hook

Get them talking about who runs their website, Google profile and socials. Vague? Try once more, then go to the Offer.

3
Offer

Objection → use the matching reply below. Multiple sites → Scale line, don't quote Core. Interested → Close.

4
Close

Yes → book the 15-minute walkthrough now, log "Call booked". Hesitant → send Email 1 straight after, log "Sent info". No → log "Not interested".

5
No answer

Leave the voicemail, send Email 1 within the hour, log "Left voicemail", follow up with Email 2 in ~5 working days.

Before you dial

  • Know the business type so the opener and hook are relevant.
  • Look at their website, Google profile and socials. Find one specific observation and put it in the prospect box above.
  • Have the Outreach Tracker open so you can log the outcome straight away.

Cold Emails

Three-touch sequence: initial, follow-up after ~5 working days, final nudge ~5 days later. Stop the moment they reply or book.

Rules for this sequence

  • Personalise the sector wording and add one specific observation about their online presence every time. Generic reads as spam.
  • Never send Email 2 or 3 if they've replied at all, even "not interested". Respect it and take them out of active outreach.
  • Tick "We have a case study" above once Help U Property's write-up exists; Email 1 then includes the proof line.
  • Log every send and reply in the Outreach Tracker's Status and Last Contact columns.
