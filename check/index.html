What we check

  • Security: whether your site loads over a secure connection, so browsers don't warn visitors off.
  • Speed: how fast your server responds and how heavy the page is for someone on mobile data.
  • Phones: whether the site is set up to fit a phone screen, where most local searches happen.
  • Google: your page title, description, main heading and business details that Google can show in search and Maps.
  • Enquiries: whether visitors can get an instant answer, an enquiry form or tap-to-call, and whether you can see your visitor numbers.

We check the one page you enter, usually your homepage. We don't store the page itself.
